SEC. 02 // HOW IT WORKS

One Frame, Five Networks, Two Fusers

Each dashcam frame fans out to five nano-scale networks running concurrently in 6 GB. Two classical-CV fusers turn raw detections into stable, usable output. Frame-skipping (heavy models every 3rd–6th frame, cached between) is what buys the frame rate.

SEC. 06 // MODEL STACK

Five Models — Three We Trained

All TensorRT FP16, ~3M parameters each, running concurrently on a laptop RTX 3050 6GB. Three were trained for this project on real public datasets; two are pretrained backbones.

route
TRAINED · SEGMENTATION

ego_seg — Ego-Lane

YOLOv8n-seg on 8k→28k real BDD100K ego-lane masks. Outputs the lane you're in, then lane-centre offset + LDW. Scaling data lifted mAP50-95 0.63→0.81.

BDD100Kmask mAP50 0.972
signpost
TRAINED · DETECTION

signs — Traffic Signs

YOLOv8n on merged GTSDB + 7.5k Indian dashcam signs. 4 super-classes (prohibitory/mandatory/danger/other) — what shape+colour reliably support, no saturated name-classifier.

GTSDB + IndianmAP50 ~0.91
traffic
TRAINED · DETECTION

light_state — Signals

YOLOv8n on Bosch Small Traffic Lights — detects state directly (R/Y/G/OFF), replacing the pixel heuristic. Honest weak spots: yellow-sparse data + US-domain gap, mitigated by the temporal tracker.

BoschmAP50 ~0.58
directions_car
PRETRAINED · DETECTION

yolov8n — Vehicles

COCO YOLOv8n: car/motorcycle/bus/truck, plus traffic-light candidate boxes as a fallback for the tracker. Re-exported FP16 (166→86 MB) — that alone lifted the pipeline from ~13 to ~23 FPS.

COCOFP16 · 16 ms

YOLOP (hustvl) via torch.hub — only its two segmentation heads exported. Drivable-area mask + lane-line pixels that refine the ego-lane mask as a secondary cue.

SEC. 08 // DESIGN DECISIONS

What We Rejected — and the Measured Reason Why

The choices that matter for a technical review. Every alternative below was actually tried or seriously evaluated, and rejected on evidence — not opinion.

TaskAlternative triedMeasured resultVerdict
Ego-laneOpenCV heuristic corridorIoU 0.06, finds lane 4% of framesRejected
Ego-laneUFLD (off-the-shelf, CULane)Placed lanes in the sky — camera-geometry domain gapRejected
Ego-laneIDD Indian lane modelmask mAP50 0.14 (label-mapping issue)Kept out; BDD default
Ego-laneYOLOv8n-seg on BDD masksmask mAP50 0.972✓ Adopted
SignsGTSRB 43-class classifier (2-stage)96.8% but SATURATED — conf 1.00 on unseen signsRejected
SignsDetector on synthetic paste-ups99.3% synthetic, ~57% real presenceRejected
SignsSingle-stage, 4 super-classes, real datamAP50 ~0.91 held-out✓ Adopted
LightsBDD-YOLO light setCollapses colour → single class, loses stateRejected
LightsBosch (native R/Y/G/OFF)mAP50 ~0.58 + temporal tracker✓ Adopted

The thesis in one row: UFLD, a strong published lane model, was useless on our camera geometry — which is exactly why we trained on real target-domain data. And a classifier that can't tell you it's uncertain (GTSRB at conf 1.00 on unseen signs) is worse than a coarser model that's honest.

SEC. 10 // LIMITATIONS & ROADMAP

What's Weak, and What We'd Build Next

Stated plainly — a system's honesty about its own limits is part of the engineering.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
  • • Signs: localisation solid, but danger↔other confusion; mandatory/prohibitory weak (data imbalance).
  • • Lights: low-confidence on Indian signals (Bosch domain gap); yellow the weakest class.
  • • Ego-lane: BDD-trained; Indian IDD variant scored only 0.14, so BDD stays default. Severe occlusion is the weak case.
  • • Night: traffic-light recall drops to ~⅓ of daytime — known field failure mode.
  • • Light association is grid-cell, not IoU — two very close lights can merge.
  • • 1080p is CPU-bound (decode dominates); 720p is the sweet spot.
ROADMAP (PRIORITISED)
  1. 1. Indian-signal traffic-light fine-tune — biggest lever: raises confidence, tightens the gate, fixes night.
  2. 2. Rebalance the sign dataset — add Indian prohibitory/mandatory to fix danger↔other.
  3. 3. Fix or drop ego_seg_idd — diagnose the IDD label mapping so an Indian lane model can beat BDD.
  4. 4. IoU-based light tracking — replace grid-cell association.
